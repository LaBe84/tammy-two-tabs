# Tammy v2 visual direction

Approved direction carried into the prototype:

- Midnight blue foundation, not Yves Klein blue.
- Soft gold accents rather than purple or bright orange.
- Serious intelligence-workbench tone rather than chatbot-demo styling.
- Left rail for current/recent work.
- Central reasoning workspace with four modes: Analyse, Compare, Challenge, Decide.
- Right rail shows the context Tammy is actually using: work items, source authority, overlap and contradiction.
- Notion remains the programme dashboard; this interface is the working/conversation surface.
- Complex orchestration stays underneath the interface.
